Asphalt 02 (Rob Tuytel): https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02
  1K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness JPEG maps. Original map registration
  and 3 metre surface scale retained. All files are served from this game.
Venice Sunset: https://polyhaven.com/a/venice_sunset
  Source mirrored in the official Three.js r180 car-material example.
Kloppenheim 06 Pure Sky: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky
Dikhololo Night: https://polyhaven.com/a/dikhololo_night
  1K linear-radiance HDR sources box-filtered to 512x256 and RGBE/RLE encoded
  using tools/prepare-lighting-probes.mjs. Runtime bounds captured radiance peaks
  before PMREM convolution; an authored directional light supplies the main key.
  These are illumination probes. The finite illustrated background plates are
  separate existing artwork, not reconstructions of the probe locations.

No runtime requests to Poly Haven or an external CDN are required.
