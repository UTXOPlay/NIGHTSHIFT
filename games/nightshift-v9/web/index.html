    Driving guide

    Hold your accelerator to build speed, release to coast and brake before a tight bend. Use the handbrake through a bend, then release and recover your line for a clean-exit bonus. Style events require a target in each of four sectors. On touch, push the stick up to accelerate and sideways to steer. The handbrake icon breaks rear grip. Pull straight down to brake, then reverse after stopping. Keep the stick down while steering to back toward an opening. On keyboard, use your REVERSE binding to back away from a blocked lane; green road chevrons mark a safe opening.

    Use the key shown on each ability. Counter incoming energy attacks when a red lane warns you. An unprotected heavy traffic impact ends the race.

    EMP needs a lock and two ammo. Shockwave reaches two nearby rivals. Rear mines defend your trailing line. Drift, draft and close passes recharge boost.
