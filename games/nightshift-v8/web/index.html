    Keyboard controls

    Select any key to rebind it. Changes save on this device.

    Find your line.

    Choose QWERTY, Colemak or your own bindings from the keyboard icon. Arrow keys always steer. Use the thumb stick on mobile. Hold your drift key, or pull the stick down while steering, to drift. Drifting, slipstreaming and close passes charge nitrous.

    The boost, fire and counter keys are shown on the HUD and update when you change layout. Counter jams an incoming attack. A red lane warns where a rival will fire: move away or counter before it fills.

    Shields absorb collisions. The next collision without a shield ends the race. A boost ram also spends collision armor. Two energy hits temporarily disable systems; a short recovery window prevents repeated hits. The jammer blocks energy attacks, not traffic. Checkpoints replenish equipment.

    EMP locks in 0.6 seconds and spends two ammo for a disabling hit. Wait for a rival guard to expire. Shockwave costs two ammo and reaches at most two racers within 18 m. Rear mines arm in 0.3 seconds, last 12 seconds, and disable on an unblocked hit. Amber map markers show active contract targets; new targets activate at route milestones. Each assigned target must be disabled separately.

    Season 8 starts a separate career. Previous signed careers remain with their original verifier.

    Finish events to earn Relay Credits. Buy cars and upgrade power, grip and capacitor in the garage. Credits have no cash value. Race rewards and purchases are saved to your account.

    Fictional Nervos City. Myelin verifies off-chain replays; no CKB settlement. This release is solo racing with AI crews.
